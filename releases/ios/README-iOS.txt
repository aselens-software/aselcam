                      AselCam iOS Distribution

TWO INSTALLATION OPTIONS ARE PROVIDED:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 1: SIDELOAD THE PRE-BUILT .IPA PACKAGE (No Mac/Xcode needed!)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
File: AselCam-v1.2-iOS.ipa

You can install this .ipa file on your iPhone or iPad using any sideloading
tool:
1. Sideloadly (Windows / Mac - Recommended):
   - Download Sideloadly from https://sideloadly.io
   - Connect your iPhone via USB.
   - Drag and drop "AselCam-v1.2-iOS.ipa" into Sideloadly.
   - Enter your Apple ID and click "Start".
2. AltStore (Windows / Mac):
   - Install AltServer and open AltStore on your device.
   - Tap "+" and choose "AselCam-v1.2-iOS.ipa".
3. TrollStore / Scarlet:
   - Open the .ipa file directly in the app.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 2: OPEN & RUN IN XCODE (Mac / Developers)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Archive: AselCam-v1.2-iOS-Xcode.zip

1. Unzip "AselCam-v1.2-iOS-Xcode.zip".
2. Double-click "AselCam.xcodeproj" to open in Xcode.
3. Select your development team under "Signing & Capabilities".
4. Plug in your iPhone and press "Cmd + R" (Run) to deploy!
