========================================================================
                      AselCam Studio - Windows Edition (x64)
========================================================================

STANDALONE PORTABLE RELEASE:
This distribution is completely self-contained. It includes the full
Windows runtime, PyQt6, NumPy, and Direct Virtual Camera & Audio Drivers.
No Python or third-party software (OBS Studio) is required!

HOW TO RUN:
1. Double-click "AselCam.exe" to launch AselCam Studio.
2. (Optional) Run "CREATE_DESKTOP_SHORTCUT.bat" to create a shortcut
   directly on your Windows Desktop.

DIRECT VIRTUAL CAMERA & MICROPHONE (AUTOMATED SETUP):
When AselCam launches, it automatically sets up and activates its direct
virtual camera and microphone devices:
- Virtual Camera: DirectShow virtual camera device recognized immediately
  by Zoom, Discord, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Skype, and browsers.
- Virtual Microphone: Direct audio driver that routes your smartphone's
  studio microphone with ultra-low latency directly into all voice chat
  and meeting applications.
- Zero configuration needed: Setup completes automatically on launch!

USB (ADB) ULTRA LOW-LATENCY MODE:
1. Enable "USB Debugging" (Developer Options) on your Android phone.
2. Connect your phone via USB cable.
3. AselCam will automatically detect the device and start streaming.
